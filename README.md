# JalanSelamat5G

**Every rider a sensor. Every second counts.**

A live 5G road-safety loop for motorcyclists in Cyberjaya. Roadside AI cameras and riders' phones feed an edge server inside Cyberjaya's 5G network, which warns drivers and riders of junction conflicts, alerts MERS 999 automatically after a crash, and shows the council which junction to fix first.

Team **YO5G**, Universiti Malaya · Cyberjaya 5G Future Talent Innovation Challenge 2026 · Track 2: 5G Connected & Safe Last-Mile Mobility

The prototype is a browser simulation of four scenarios: junction conflict warning, false alarm and hazard confirmation, crash detection with a simulated 999 alert, and the council hotspot view. Timings shown are design targets, and council figures are example data. It does not connect to real emergency services or traffic signals.
